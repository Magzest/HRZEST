## Technical Notes

- Plan is stored in the `admin_users.plan` column (`basic`, `medium`, `premium`).
- Default plan for new accounts: `basic`.
- Employee cap is enforced server-side on employee creation.
- Feature access is enforced via `@require_plan()` decorator in `blueprints/plan_guard.py`.
- Daily email reports are generated by `blueprints/daily_report.py` (APScheduler, 11:59 PM daily).
