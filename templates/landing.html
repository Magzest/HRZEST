What's Included

Every HR function your company actually runs

Not a bundle of disconnected tools — one schema-isolated workspace per company, covering the full employee lifecycle.

Attendance & Shifts

Kiosk check-in by QR, face or fingerprint, geofenced to your office, with shift scheduling, break tracking and swap requests.

Leave & Payroll

Leave requests, balances and holidays feed straight into full Indian payroll — Basic/HRA/PF/PT/TDS payslips, computed automatically.

Onboarding & Offer Letters

Reusable onboarding checklists and offer letters candidates can accept from a single emailed link — no account required.

Performance Reviews

Quarterly reviews, KPI tracking and a 9-box talent matrix that ties directly into hike and bonus decisions.

AI-Powered HR

Resume screening, an employee helpdesk chatbot with automatic ticket escalation, and interview-note synthesis.

Security & Compliance

WebAuthn passkeys, TOTP MFA, a dedicated SecOps console and an audit-log explorer — separate credentials for every role.
